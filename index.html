Research Objectives

Project Objectives of A.N.I.H.A.N.

Grounded in the realities of Filipino agriculture, this platform was designed to address systemic challenges in the local produce supply chain.

General Objective

To design and develop a web-based agricultural coordination platform — A.N.I.H.A.N. — that streamlines the local produce supply chain by connecting farmers, agricultural workers, and local buyers through a centralized harvest timeline calendar and direct pre-ordering system.

  • 01
    Eliminate Post-Harvest Spoilage and Glut Crashes

    Enable advance pre-orders so farmers secure committed buyers before harvesting, preventing rotting crops and distress farmgate price dumping in the local market.

  • 02
    Guarantee Fair and Direct Farmgate Pricing

    Bypass predatory middleman layers by connecting growers directly with buyers — for crops, livestock, poultry, and eggs — with transparent negotiated contracts and open market rates.

  • 03
    Inclusive Platform for All Farmers, Including Non-Literate Growers

    Agricultural Extension Workers register accounts on behalf of farmers who cannot read or write, ensuring zero exclusion from the digital marketplace.

  • 04
    Actionable Municipal Market Interventions

    Aggregate planting data into 3 predictive graphs, alerting LGUs and Ag Workers of impending over-harvests so they can trigger Kadiwa market dispatch, cold storage, or flash sales before produce is wasted.

  • 05
    Full Fulfillment Transparency for Crops and Livestock

    Provide explicit Pick-up and Delivery options for every listing — whether fresh vegetables, live cattle, native chicken, duck eggs, or native pigs — with a 5-step Shopee-style order tracking system.

Target Users

Who Uses A.N.I.H.A.N.?

Three distinct user roles, each with a dedicated interface and purpose within the agricultural coordination ecosystem.

Farmer / Grower

Magsasaka — Sell Your Harvest

List your crops, livestock (cattle, pigs, goats, chicken), and eggs with pick-up or delivery options. Receive pre-orders from verified buyers and track each order through the 5-step fulfillment system.

Buyer / Vendor

Mamimili — Pre-Order Fresh Produce

Browse certified crops, live animals, and farm eggs directly from Central Luzon growers. Pre-order with your preferred fulfillment mode and track delivery or pick-up status in real time.

Barangay Ag Worker

Manggagawa — Low-Tech Assistance

Assists farmers with low technology literacy to register accounts, access them via secure Pass Code, list harvests/livestock on their behalf, and track their distribution.

LGU Agriculture Office

LGU Admin — Municipal Surveillance

Manage all accounts with search & filter, approve/disapprove & edit credentials, monitor 3 predictive Over-Harvest graphs, and supervise municipal-wide distribution.

Platform Workflow

How A.N.I.H.A.N. Works

From farm to table — a simple, transparent three-step process.

1

Farmers Post Their Harvest

Growers register their crops, livestock, and eggs with price, available quantity, fulfillment mode (pick-up or delivery), and harvest readiness dates. Photos and ratings build buyer trust.

2

Buyers Browse and Pre-Order

Verified buyers explore the marketplace, filter by category, rate and review produce, and place pre-orders directly from the grower — no middlemen, no hidden fees.

3

Fulfill and Track Every Order

Both farmer and buyer track every order through 5 transparent steps — from confirmation and harvest to transit, delivery, and final handover — just like a modern logistics platform.
