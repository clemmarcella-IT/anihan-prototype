How Barangay Low-Tech Farmer Assistance Works:

1. Register Farmer: Encode the profile of a smallholder farmer who has low technology literacy. The system automatically assigns a secure Pass Code.
2. LGU Verification: Newly registered accounts are submitted to the LGU Municipal Admin for official review and approval.
3. Sell & Log In: Once approved, use the farmer's Pass Code to one-click log in to their account, post their harvested vegetables or livestock, and monitor incoming buyer pre-orders!
