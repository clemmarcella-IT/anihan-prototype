The 3 Over-Harvest Predictive Graphs & Harvest Timeline

Data-driven coordination to monitor impending harvest gluts across Guimba and neighboring clusters, identify farmgate crash risks, and trigger proactive municipal interventions.

Graph 1: Municipal Supply vs. Market Demand (Surplus & Deficit)

Identifies impending harvest gluts before crops rot in the field. When Supply exceeds Demand by >40%, an Over-Harvest Alert is raised.

Graph 2: Spoilage & Post-Harvest Waste Risk

Percentage of total output vulnerable to spoilage based on perishability index and uncommitted volume.

Graph 3: 30-Day Farmgate Price Volatility

Monitors price plunges during glut seasons versus price stability achieved via direct pre-ordering contracts.
